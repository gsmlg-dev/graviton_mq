## Selected profile and reading the ledger

Operational client/broker roles use MQTT 5, QoS 0/1/2, ordinary subscriptions, TCP/TLS, bounded resources and explicit host ownership. The broker can negotiate Maximum QoS 0/1. Both advertise Topic Alias Maximum 0; the broker advertises Shared Subscriptions and Subscription Identifiers unavailable. Enhanced authentication/AUTH and WebSocket transport are not accepted operational profiles. Client-side shared/ID wire grammar and peer capability gates do not qualify shared-session persistence or a shared broker scheduler. Memory mode and opt-in local durability have separate loss guarantees.

Client/broker applicability means the normative obligation for that role: `supported`, `conditional`, `rejected optional`, `not applicable`, or `excluded transport`. A peer checker can provide useful evidence even when the obligation belongs to the other role. `SOURCE_AND_REGRESSION` means source plus a relevant bounded regression was located, **not PASS for every clause or byte permutation**. `SOURCE_ONLY` explicitly lacks a direct exact-branch regression. `NOT_EMITTED` and `CONDITION_INACTIVE` explain a satisfied/inactive predicate rather than demanding an optional feature. `OPTIONAL_REJECTED` records an explicit refusal boundary. Independent/recovery references are existing evidence, not executions performed during this review.
